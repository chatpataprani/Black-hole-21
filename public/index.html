BLACK HOLE 21

the number strategy game with a gravity problem

How to play

  1. 21 circles form a pyramid of six rows.
  2. Players alternate — pick an empty circle, then a number 1–10.
  3. Once placed, a number is locked in forever.
  4. After 20 moves, exactly one circle is left empty. It becomes the Black Hole.
  5. The Black Hole pulls in every number touching it — the pull follows fixed adjacency, not distance on screen.
  6. Each player's absorbed numbers are added up.
  7. Lowest total wins. Equal totals mean a draw.
